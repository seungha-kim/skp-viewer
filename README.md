Experimental SketchUp viewer

![screenshot](image.png)

## Features

- **Laptop-Friendly Camera Controls**:
  - **Orbit / Rotate**: Left-click and drag anywhere on the 3D canvas to rotate around the entire model.
  - **Pan**: Hold **Shift + Left-click and drag** (or use Middle-click drag) to move the camera up, down, left, or right.
  - **Zoom**: Scroll the **Mouse Wheel** (or use a two-finger vertical swipe on a trackpad) to zoom in and out.
- **Click-to-Focus Navigation**: 
  - Clicking on a room or object name in the left-hand **Hierarchy Panel** will instantly focus and move the camera to that specific 3D component.
- **Layer Toggles**:
  - Use the right-hand **Tags Panel** to toggle the visibility of specific SketchUp layers.

## Formatter

- [clang-format](https://clang.llvm.org/docs/ClangFormat.html) for C++
